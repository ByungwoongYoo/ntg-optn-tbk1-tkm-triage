# CAMI support inquiry — draft only, not sent

**To:** support@cami-challenge.org  
**Subject:** Clarification before one frozen CAMI III Longitudinal Human Gut assembly submission

Dear CAMI team,

I am an independent researcher preparing one preregistered, frozen assembly workflow for the CAMI III Longitudinal Human Gut assembly challenge. Before accepting the restricted-data agreement or staging any active data, I would be grateful for clarification on the following operational points.

1. For the accepted “patient-specific cross-sample assemblies” mode, should a participant upload:
   - nine separate FASTA files / nine separate submissions, one for each individual;
   - one archive containing nine FASTA files; or
   - one combined FASTA containing all nine patient-specific assemblies with globally unique contig IDs?

2. May the exact frozen candidate and one frozen strongest baseline be submitted as separate named methods for the same data mode? If yes, what is the maximum number of submissions, and may a technically invalid upload be replaced without changing the method?

3. The challenge page permits a first-ten-sample route. Because the published sample-to-individual mapping means the first ten indices do not form complete four-timepoint individuals, is that route eligible only for global ten-sample cross-assembly / ten single-sample assemblies, or can it validly evaluate a longitudinal patient-specific method?

4. Can an independent researcher register and access the active dataset using a personal email address, or is a university/institutional email mandatory? I will personally review and accept any agreement; no automated agent will do so.

5. Under the active-data embargo, are participant-derived assemblies and logs also prohibited from public repositories until CAMI/data-contributor release? May they be retained privately for audit and submission reproducibility?

6. When are official assembly evaluation results expected to be communicated, and which metrics/versions will determine the official CAMI III assembly comparison? The public upload page currently identifies MetaQUAST 5.2.0, while active-challenge online evaluation is disabled.

7. Is there a preferred method for providing a private, unpublished Docker image or repository to the CAMI team for reproducibility?

The workflow will be frozen before active-data access, will use the published nine-individual mapping, and will not be retuned after observing active outputs or scores.

Thank you for your guidance.
