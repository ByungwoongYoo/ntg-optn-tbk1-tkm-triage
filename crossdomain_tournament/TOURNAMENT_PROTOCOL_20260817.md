# Cross-domain exact-witness open-problem tournament

Freeze date: 2026-08-17

## Goal

Search across medicine/history/Korean medicine/archaeology/philosophy/chemistry/biology and adjacent exact computational problems for results that terminate in a concrete witness or machine-checkable certificate rather than a merely better average benchmark score.

## Anti-cherry-picking rule

Each track must first pass an objective smoke test on hidden or previously known truth before any claimed new discovery is pursued. Positive exploratory findings must be attacked with a stronger falsification. A track is stopped when its prespecified witness criterion cannot be evaluated with public data, requires unavailable physical access, or fails its hidden-truth smoke test.

## Tracks

### T1 Nineveh Medical Encyclopaedia fragment joins
Witness: a previously unrecorded direct or indirect join candidate between medical tablet fragments, supported by independent text/geometry evidence; definitive direct join ultimately requires museum physical/3D verification.
Smoke test: hide known joins and recover them from public transliteration/image/metadata.
Priority: highest medical-history track.

### T2 Uibangyuchwi lost-medical-text reconstruction
Witness: a previously unreported passage attributable to a lost cited medical work, with exact source locations and independent parallel-text support.
Smoke test: hide already reconstructed Gangibang/Lisangsokdanbang passages and recover source attribution computationally.
Priority: highest Korean-medicine track.

### T3 DECRYPT historical medical/alchemical ciphers
Witness: ciphertext + key/plaintext + re-encryption consistency, preferably a record with medical/alchemical context.
Smoke test: hide plaintext/key of solved public records and recover it.
Blocker rule: records requiring authentication may be catalogued but not claimed solved without accessible transcription/image.

### T4 Positive implicational logic u4
Witness: a machine-checkable derivation that u4 is a single axiom, or a finite/infinite countermodel / saturated clause certificate proving it is not.
Smoke test: reproduce published u1-u3 refutations with Vampire/E/iProver before attacking u4.
Priority: exact philosophy/logic track.

### T5 RePAIR Pompeii fresco reassembly
Witness: a previously unrecorded fragment match, supported by 2D/3D compatibility; definitive archaeological claim requires expert/physical confirmation.
Smoke test: hide known ground-truth fragment relationships from the ~1,000 reconstructed subset and recover them.

### T6 Vesuvius Challenge
Witness: newly readable ink/text region or title satisfying current challenge verification rules.
Smoke test: reproduce publicly verified ink/text on released segments with open models before unseen surfaces.
Compute stop: do not claim progress from visual plausibility alone.

### T7 CASP17 blind structural prediction
Witness: prediction submitted before experimental structure release and later objectively scored against hidden truth.
Smoke test: reproduce prior released targets using the same pipeline.
External-action boundary: official submission/registration is only performed with user authorization if account creation or terms acceptance is required.

### T8 Exact finite-combinatorics control
Primary candidate: B2[2] subset of Z/100Z (13 vs 14) or another current exact finite target after literature-status verification.
Witness: construction or independently checked UNSAT/proof certificate.
Purpose: maintain at least one fully machine-verifiable track with no expert confirmation bottleneck.

## Triage scores

For every track record:
1. open-status confidence (0-3)
2. public-data access (0-3)
3. objective hidden/known truth for smoke testing (0-3)
4. free-compute feasibility (0-3)
5. witness definiteness (0-3)
6. novelty headroom (0-3)
7. medical/Korean-medicine relevance bonus (0-2)

Maximum 20.

## Promotion rule

A track advances from smoke to pursuit only if:
- open status is verified from primary/authoritative sources;
- required public data are actually retrievable;
- hidden-known-truth smoke test beats a simple baseline and returns at least one exact true positive;
- the claimed new target set excludes already known/published witnesses.

## Final handoff

For every survivor create:
- problem statement and primary sources;
- exact witness criterion;
- downloaded-data manifest and hashes;
- smoke-test code/results;
- known-result exclusion list;
- strongest candidate(s), including negative evidence;
- next one to three decisive experiments;
- explicit blockers requiring expert/physical/account access.

The Pro handoff should receive only survivors plus an appendix of failed tracks, not a list of untested ideas.
